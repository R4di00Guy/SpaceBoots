About us

SpaceBoots was born on September 15, 2025, in Gorzyce. Although we are a young brand, our energy and ambitions reach for the stars. We were founded out of a passion for modern design and the conviction that high-quality footwear shouldn't just be an accessory, it should be the foundation of your daily success. We are a team of 7 visionaries who decided to defy gravity and challenge the boredom of the footwear market. Each of us brings a different expertise to SpaceBoots from technological design and traditional shoemaking craftsmanship to an obsessive focus on customer service. We are united by one thing: local pride and global ambitions. We chose Gorzyce as our operational base because we believe that this is where, away from the hustle and bustle of big corporations, the best ideas are born. In our creation process, we utilize materials of the future that guarantee durability even in the most demanding conditions. We constantly test new solutions to provide your feet with the support they deserve every single day. We believe that authenticity and transparency in production are key to building a lasting bond with our community. We invite you to join us on this journey, where every step is proof that nothing is impossible.
